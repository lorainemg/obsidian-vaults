Topic: [[Week 2 Feature Engineering, Transformation and Selection]]

----
The art of feature engineering tries to improve your model's ability to learn while reducing the compute resources it requires. For this, the folliwing steps must bet met:
- The original features must be transformed or projected to a new space and or combinations of your features.
- Objective function must be properly tuned to make your model is heading in the right direction and that is consistent with your feature engineering.
- The model can be updated by adding new features from the set of data that is available,
- This tends to be an iterative process that gradually improves your results
